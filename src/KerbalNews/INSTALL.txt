Kerbal News @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

A news channel in flight: a kerbal anchor at his desk talks over what your flight does,
from a small window in a corner of the screen (it comes on with static), with captions; or
on the whole screen, where he moves aside to show your flight live beside him when
something is happening. His voice is made with ElevenLabs.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone 0.11 or later, the base it stands on, which is a separate download:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
  Copy Keystone's folder (Keystone) into your KSP's GameData folder, and the KerbalNews
  folder from this download beside it, and start the game. Without Keystone the game does
  not load this mod. One copy of Keystone serves every mod that uses it. To remove this
  mod, delete the KerbalNews folder.

USE
  Switch the channel on in the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar,
  or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K elsewhere), under Kerbal News: that shows nothing by
  itself. Then in flight Alt-N (Option-N on a Mac) brings the anchor's window up and takes
  it down again. With the pointer over its window, the button at the window's top right
  puts him on the whole screen; the button at the screen's top right puts him back. Also
  under Kerbal News: which corner his window is in and how big it is, how much he talks,
  his voice's loudness, and captions.
