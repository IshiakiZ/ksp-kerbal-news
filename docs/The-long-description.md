# Kerbal News: the long description

The front page is kept short. This is the mod at length, and what was tried on each system.

A news channel in flight: a kerbal anchor at his desk talks over what your flight does.

With the channel switched on, the hotkey brings up a small window in a corner of the screen, with static first, as the crew's
portraits have: then the anchor in his newsroom, at his desk with a microphone. What happens sets off his lines: the rocket on the
pad, lift-off, staging, heights passed, space, orbit, a new world, re-entry, the parachutes, touchdown or splashdown, a crash, a
kerbal stepping outside, a docking, a flag, science, fuel running low. In the quiet between, he talks about what the flight is
doing (time warp, a coast in orbit, the night side and the sunrise, a rover's drive, a plane's cruise), or of the studio's own:
jokes, facts about the worlds, viewers' letters, a word from the sponsors. About 1,500 lines, a caption under each, and his mouth
moving with his voice. Taken down by the hotkey, he signs off before the window folds; and if the craft is recovered while he is
on, he says so at the space centre.

With the pointer over the window, a button at its top right puts him on the whole screen. There, when something is
happening, he moves aside and your flight is shown beside him, live, as a newscast puts its pictures beside the anchor.
The craft still answers its keys meanwhile, and the camera the mouse; the game's own interface is put away until the
button at the top right puts him back in his window.

**Not released: the first version, being finished.** All 1,504 of its lines are voiced (ElevenLabs, the voice Robert). It is
kept out of the owner's own game until they want it there.

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x. Needs [Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone), a separate download
([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone)): this mod does not load without it.

## What was tried

* **Mac (OpenGL), the development build, at the pad:** 2026-10-18 and 19, with stand-in voices: the window, its static, the
  whole screen with the flight beside him, the lines by event, his face and jaw. 2026-10-20, 17:39-17:44: the first 1,030
  voiced lines play and his jaw follows them; what the quiet spells were filled with suited the pad, a coast in low orbit and
  time warp. 2026-10-21, 16:02-16:18: three of each kind of the day's 427 new lines (36) play, his jaw following and each
  caption right; at the pad the quiet spells come out by their shares (of 200: quiet 68, joke 48, fact 31, studio 22, mail 17,
  ad 14); Alt-N signs him off and folds the window as he finishes (3.5 s), pressed again folds it at once; recovered from the
  pad, his recovery line is said at the space centre over the Mission Summary, in his voice and with its caption. 16:33-16:40:
  his mouth rig, held in seven shapes from the front and from three quarters and pictured through two lines: even on both
  sides, opening and closing with the syllables (the corners first drew in so far that his teeth showed through the cheek in
  three quarters: less now).
* **Windows 11 (Direct3D 11), 2026-10-20, through its development switches:** switched on with nothing shown, its window with
  static and then the anchor, a line said, the whole screen with the flight beside him, back, down; the craft's name in its
  banner mended (a stock craft's showed as "#autoLOC_501232"). The Alt-N key itself not pressed there.
* **Linux:** never run.
