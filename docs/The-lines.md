# The lines

1,504 lines, each spoken once by ElevenLabs in one voice (Robert, an American broadcaster: the owner's pick of four on
2026-10-18) and kept as a recording: 115 minutes of speech. Each has a caption, its words without the delivery marks.
For an event a line is picked by chance, never one of the last three said for it.

**Big moments** are said even on "only the big moments" and wait up to 20 seconds behind a line being said; **the rest** wait
8 seconds and are left out on that setting.
