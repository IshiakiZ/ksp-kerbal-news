# Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac), under **Kerbal News**.

| Setting | What it does | As it comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| News channel | On: the channel is ready, and nothing is shown yet. In flight, Alt-N (Option-N on a Mac) brings the anchor's window up, and takes it down again after his sign-off. Off: no window, whatever the key. | off |
| His box | Which corner of the screen his window is in: bottom right, bottom left, top right, top left. Kept clear of the game's own toolbar, the crew's portraits and the clock. | bottom right |
| Box size | How wide his window is, as a share of the screen's width (0.15 to 0.5); it is 16 by 9. | 0.28 |
| How much he talks | Only the big moments (lift-off, orbit, a landing, a crash and the like); normal; chatty (more, sooner after the last line, and a quiet spell filled every 45 seconds instead of 90). | normal |
| Voice | How loud he is, 0 to 1. The game's own voice volume applies as well. | 0.9 |
| Captions | What he says, written under him (on the whole screen and in the small window alike, and at the space centre after a recovery). | on |

The window's own button (top right, with the pointer over it) puts him on the whole screen and back. Nothing about the window
is saved: a game starts with it down.
