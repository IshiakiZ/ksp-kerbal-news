# Kerbal News

A news channel in flight in Kerbal Space Program 1: a kerbal anchor at his desk talks over what your flight does, in about
1,500 lines in one voice, with a caption under each and his mouth moving with his voice. The short version is on the
repository's front page.

* [The long description](The-long-description.md): everything the front page says at more length, and what was tried on each system
* [The lines](The-lines.md): what sets each kind off, how many there are, the quiet spells, the voice
* [How it works](How-it-works.md): the newsroom, the window and the whole screen, his mouth, the line after a recovery
* [Settings](Settings.md)
* [Limits](Limits.md), and which systems it works on
* [Building it yourself](Building.md), the voices and the suit included
