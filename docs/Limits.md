# Limits

* **One voice, in English.** The lines were spoken by ElevenLabs in one voice (Robert, an American broadcaster) and are fixed
  recordings: nothing is spoken on your machine, and nothing is sent anywhere. A craft's name is shown in the banner, never
  said.
* **The download is mostly voice:** about 75 MB of Ogg Vorbis (mono, 115 minutes of speech) of its 77 MB. Each line is read
  from disk when it is said, whole, and let go when the next one is.
* **The line after a recovery** is said only for a recovery from the flight (the game's Recover button) with his window up;
  one from the tracking station has none.
* **Sign-off:** taking the window down by the hotkey lets him finish his sign-off (a few seconds); switching the channel off in
  the Keystone window folds it at once.
* **A flight only.** The newsroom is not shown at the space centre or in the editors (after a recovery, only his voice and
  its caption).
* **Systems:** made and tried on a Mac (OpenGL). Run on Windows 11 (Direct3D 11) through its development switches on
  2026-10-20; the Alt-N key itself not pressed there. Linux never run.
